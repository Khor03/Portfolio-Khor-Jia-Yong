About

Grounded in fundamentals, curious about systems

I am an Electronic Engineering student at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) with a strong interest in electronics, embedded systems and emerging technologies. I enjoy transforming ideas into functional solutions, from circuit design and PCB development to embedded firmware, hardware integration and AI-IoT implementation.

Through my academic coursework, internships and personal projects, I have developed hands-on experience in electronics development, microcontrollers, sensors, IoT systems, programming and troubleshooting. I am motivated by projects that combine hardware and software to solve practical real-world problems. I value continuous learning, teamwork, problem-solving and adaptability and I look forward to contributing my skills while continuing to grow in a challenging and forward-thinking organisation.

Outside of engineering, I maintain an active lifestyle through gym workouts, strength training and swimming, which have strengthened my discipline, consistency, determination and focus. I also enjoy exploring different sweets and desserts, especially cakes, as I appreciate discovering new flavours, local specialties and the creativity behind different cuisines.
